# The World — Friday 27 November 2026

[![news-image-20261127](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261127.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261127)

One image summing up the world's news of **Friday 27 November 2026**:
two strikes shake the Gulf (a drone attack in Kuwait kills two Italian
technicians; a projectile hits Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery, killing a
Nepali worker); the EU summit ends with no deal on the 2028–2034 budget
or a €45bn Ukraine loan, and Spain's de Cos is picked as the next ECB
chief; Zelensky says Washington's Middle East focus delays Patriot
missiles; New Zealand's ruling coalition loses its majority in the
official count; Nvidia is put on CreditWatch Negative while China's
industrial profits jump 20.1%; Mount Mayon erupts in the Philippines;
and the US clears SpaceX to launch up to a million orbital data-centre
satellites — plus the day's briefs.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the finished
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image was drawn in the build
session with Pillow and DejaVu fonts and is deterministic: two clean
renders produce identical bytes.
